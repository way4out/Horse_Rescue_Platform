Questions

Clear answers matter.

Public visitors should know what the platform does, what information is verified, and how rescue involvement works.

Are the horses shown here real?

Public profiles are published intentionally from the rescue database. Unpublished operational records remain private.

Can I adopt or foster an animal?

Yes. Submit an interest request and the rescue team can determine the appropriate next step based on the individual animal and placement requirements.

Can I help without taking a horse?

Absolutely. Transport, veterinary and dental care, feed, supplies, volunteering, sponsorship and professional partnerships can all directly support rescue operations.

What happens after intake?

Each animal can move through transport, quarantine, assessment, veterinary care, rehabilitation, training, placement or sanctuary as appropriate.

Is private horse-care information public?

No. The public site is separated from the authenticated staff workspace. Sensitive operational records remain in the protected backend.

How do I report an urgent animal?

Use the help form with the available information. For an immediate welfare emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency or animal-control authority first.
