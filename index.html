Care model

Rescue first. Understand completely. Place responsibly.

The platform follows the whole animal-care journey rather than treating a horse as a listing: intake, transport, quarantine, veterinary care, rehabilitation, placement and follow-up.

01 · ADOPTABLE

Ready for the next chapter.

Public profiles, applications and responsible placement tracking for horses cleared for a home.

02 · EVALUATION

Still healing. Still learning.

Veterinary, behavioral, training, nutrition and rehabilitation work stay attached to the individual case.

03 · SANCTUARY

Here for life.

Lifelong records and care planning when permanent sanctuary is the appropriate welfare outcome.

The complete journey

From crisis to a safe outcome.

Every stage has a place in the workflow so urgent needs do not disappear between people, phones and spreadsheets.

01 · IDENTIFY

Source, location, urgency, photos, initial condition and immediate needs.

02 · MOVE

Transport route, carrier, departure, arrival and quarantine status.

03 · STABILIZE

Vet, dental, nutrition, medication, farrier and rehabilitation records.

04 · PLACE

Adoption, foster, sponsorship, sanctuary and follow-up.
