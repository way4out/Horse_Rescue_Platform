Questions

Clear answers matter.

Public visitors should know what the platform does, what information is verified, and how rescue involvement works.

Are the horses shown here real?

Sample records are explicitly marked as demo data. Live public profiles are pulled from the rescue database only when staff publish them.

Can I adopt or foster?

Yes. Submit an interest request and the rescue team can determine the appropriate next step based on the individual horse and placement requirements.

Can I help without taking a horse?

Absolutely. Transport, veterinary and dental care, feed, supplies, volunteering, sponsorship and professional partnerships can all directly support rescue operations.

What happens after intake?

Each horse can move through transport, quarantine, assessment, veterinary care, rehabilitation, training, placement or sanctuary as appropriate.

Is private horse-care information public?

No. The public site is separated from the authenticated staff workspace. Sensitive operational records are intended to remain in the protected backend.

How do I report an urgent horse?

Use the help form with the available information. For an immediate welfare emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency or animal-control authority first.
